How it works

Three steps, then you review

  1. Describe the task Say what plan, calculation, or document you need, and point SurvyAI at the job files.
  2. It uses the tools on this PC Signed-in hosted AI can interpret the request. Drawing, GIS, and file tools still run locally.
  3. You check the result Review the drawing, numbers, or spreadsheet in your workspace before you rely on them.

Privacy

Your workspace stays on your PC

This is the privacy notice for survyai.com and for SurvyAI Desktop. Questions: support@survyai.com.

Files

Drawings, spreadsheets, and other job files stay in the workspace folder you choose. SurvyAI does not keep a copy of that library, and it does not upload the folder as a backup.

Hosted AI

When you are signed in and use a hosted model, the task text and any file content that task needs are sent to SurvyAI’s cloud service so the model can respond. Desktop tools still run on your computer. A local model, if you turn one on, runs on this PC instead.

Account

The desktop app stores sign-in secrets with Windows DPAPI. We use your account email to sign you in, manage beta or paid access, and send account messages. Password-reset mail comes from noreply@survyai.com.

Beta signup

The first-user form stores the name, email, and any optional profession, intended use, and Windows details you submit. We use that list to grant beta access and to see which jobs to improve. We do not sell it. Joining does not turn Pro on by itself. After the beta, Pro is a paid plan. Email support to correct or delete a signup.

This website

Pages are served over HTTPS. The site stores an anonymous id in your browser so we can count visits and installer clicks. It is not your name or email, and we do not sell it.

FAQ

Before you install

What can I do in the beta?

Produce and edit AutoCAD survey plans, work with coordinates and areas, drive ArcGIS Pro when it is installed, and write schedules and spreadsheets into your workspace. You review the output before you use it.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.

Do I need AutoCAD or ArcGIS?

AutoCAD is required to produce or edit survey plans. Coordinate and document tasks can run without it. ArcGIS Pro is only needed when you ask SurvyAI to drive a GIS project.

What data is sent to hosted AI?

When you are signed in, the task text and any file content that task needs go to SurvyAI’s hosted model. The rest of the workspace is not uploaded as a backup. See Privacy.

Can I download without joining?

Yes. The installer is on this page. Joining tells us who you are so we can grant Pro for the beta. Joining does not turn Pro on by itself. After the beta, Pro is paid.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@survyai.com with what you were trying to do and what happened. You can also send feedback from the desktop app.
