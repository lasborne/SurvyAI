FAQ

Before you install

What happens after I join the first users?

We keep your name, email, profession, what you want to use SurvyAI for, and the Windows details you type. During the beta we use that list to grant Pro for the beta period and to see which survey jobs to improve. After the beta, Pro is a paid plan. Joining does not turn Pro on by itself.

Do I need AutoCAD or ArcGIS?

AutoCAD is required to produce or edit survey plans. Coordinate and document tasks can run without it. ArcGIS Pro is only needed when you ask SurvyAI to drive a GIS project.

Where do my drawings go?

CAD files, spreadsheets, and other job files stay in the workspace folder on your PC. When you are signed in, the task text — and any file content that task needs — is sent to SurvyAI’s hosted model. The desktop tools still run on your computer.

Can I download the beta without joining?

Yes. The Windows installer is on this page. Joining tells us who you are so we can grant beta Pro access and learn what to build next. After the beta, Pro is paid.

Privacy / security

Privacy & security

Survey files stay in the workspace you choose on your PC. SurvyAI does not keep a copy of your drawing library.

Sign-in secrets in the desktop app are stored with Windows DPAPI. Installers published here are checked by SHA-256 in the in-app updater before they are applied.

Hosted AI, used when you are signed in, sends that task to SurvyAI’s cloud service so the model can answer. It does not upload your workspace as a backup.

The first-user form stores only what you submit: name, email, profession, intended use, and Windows specs. We use it to grant access, improve the product, and understand how surveyors want to work. We do not sell it. Questions go to support@survyai.com.

About

Built for the survey desk

SurvyAI is developed for land surveyors and geospatial analysts who need an assistant that understands cadastral work, coordinate systems, and drafting standards — and then operates the tools already on the workstation.

The product experience is SurvyAI Desktop on Windows: a professional console for natural-language tasks, with optional cloud sign-in for hosted models, subscriptions, and credits. Files stay in your workspace; account secrets are stored with Windows DPAPI.

This site is the public face of the product: downloads, update artifacts, and support for the team shipping SurvyAI.
